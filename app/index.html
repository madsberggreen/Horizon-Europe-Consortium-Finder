research-funding plugin for Claude

Find your Horizon Europe consortium

Fill in the form, copy the request and paste it into Claude. You get matched open calls, a policy briefing, consortium partners from CORDIS and three draft pitch letters to coordinators.

Public dataUses only publicly available data: ORCID, institutional pages, CORDIS and the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

Your inputAdds your own idea and documents. They steer the profile, the policy briefing and the pitch letters, so the results can point to a field beyond your usual one.

Helps Claude find the right person when names are common.

Before you add anything

Check your institutional guidelines on AI use before uploading any data. Only upload data that you or your institution has full ownership of.

Documents (optional: rejected applications, one-pagers, concept notes)
Drop files here or click to choose PDF, Word (.docx), .txt or .md. The text is read in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste the request into Claude.
